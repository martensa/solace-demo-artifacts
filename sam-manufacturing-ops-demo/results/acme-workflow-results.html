Executive summary of the investigation

Engineering change ECO-2025-118 (“HD-22 sintered clutch disc introduction,” effective 2025-07-20) raised the EOL torque spec from 18.0 ± 0.5 Nm → 22.0 ± 0.5 Nm (current Rev C window 21.5–22.5 Nm). The ECO is RELEASED and acknowledged at Hamburg, but still PENDING — never acknowledged — at the failing plant PLANT_GRZ. Station EOL-2 in Graz is therefore still enforcing the obsolete Rev-B limits (17.5–18.5 Nm) while the line builds the new HD-22 hardware.

Telemetry

A clean level shift from ~18.0 Nm → ~22.0 Nm began 2025-07-28 06:07 UTC (last good unit 07-27 20:28), taking the station from ~1.25% to a 100% fail rate (69.6% of all L3 records overall — 181 of 260). Sustained failures begin three minutes after the first HD-22 lot issue (06:04 → 06:07 UTC). station_spec_revision is confirmed as “B” on all records. The measured 22.1 Nm is in-spec against the correct Rev-C limits and only fails against the outdated station setting — these are almost certainly false rejects.

OMS

PRD-118-4718 is on QUALITY_HOLD (1,200 units). It directly fulfils ORD-2025-30388 (ACC_TOOLMART, €226,800, DACH autumn campaign, requested 2025-08-20 — only 4 days after scheduled build end). A cascading order ORD-2025-30422 (ACC_ORIENTT, €113,400) is queued behind it on L3. Total value exposed: €340,200 / 1,800 units.

Top recommendations

  1. Update EOL-2 (L3, Graz) torque spec and calibration to 22.0 ± 0.5 Nm and acknowledge ECO-2025-118.
  2. Quarantine and re-test units rejected under the wrong spec to release PRD-118-4718 from hold.
  3. Proactively notify ACC_TOOLMART given the tight 4-day delivery buffer.
  4. Fix the systemic gap that let HD-22 lots reach the line before the ECO was closed at Graz.
